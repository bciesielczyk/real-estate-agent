# Home Search

Finding homes for sale in Poland that are worth a closer look, and learning more about them by phoning whoever advertised them.

## Language

**Property**:
A home itself: an apartment, terraced house, semi-detached house or detached house. One Property may appear in several Listings.
_Avoid_: Offer, flat, apartment (when any home type is meant)

**Listing**:
A single advertisement of a Property, published in one place by one Advertiser.
_Avoid_: Offer, ad, post

**Advertiser**:
The person or agency that published a Listing.
_Avoid_: Seller, owner (an Advertiser may be neither)

**Brief**:
What one user is looking for: criteria first described in their own words, then confirmed by them. Location is one criterion among others.
_Avoid_: Search, filter, query

**Good Listing**:
A Listing that fits a given Brief, is attractively priced and is reasonably recent.
_Avoid_: Good offer, deal
